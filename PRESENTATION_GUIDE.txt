PROJECT OVERVIEW
================================================================================
A comprehensive web-based platform designed to streamline event management 
in educational institutions. The system provides role-based access for 
Students, Teachers, and Administrators to organize, participate in, and 
manage college events efficiently.

USE CASES & SCENARIOS
================================================================================

SCENARIO 1: ANNUAL CULTURAL FEST
   Problem: Managing 500+ student registrations manually
   Solution: 
   - Teacher creates event with details and fees
   - Students register online with payment
   - System tracks registrations in real-time
   - Admin monitors overall progress
   - Automated certificate generation post-event
   
   Result: Seamless management of large-scale event

SCENARIO 2: TECHNICAL WORKSHOP SERIES
   Problem: Limited seats, need waitlist management
   Solution:
   - Set maximum participant limit
   - First-come-first-served registration
   - Automatic waitlist for overflow
   - Email notifications for confirmations
   
   Result: Fair and transparent registration process

SCENARIO 3: SPORTS TOURNAMENT
   Problem: Multiple events, team registrations, scheduling
   Solution:
   - Create multiple event entries for different sports
   - Track team-wise registrations
   - View participant lists by category
   - Generate fixture schedules
   
   Result: Organized multi-event management

SCENARIO 4: CAREER FAIR
   Problem: Coordinating with companies, student tracking
   Solution:
   - Create event with company details
   - Students register with resume upload
   - Track student-company interactions
   - Generate attendance reports for companies
   
   Result: Professional career fair execution
